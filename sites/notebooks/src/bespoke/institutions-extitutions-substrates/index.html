Glossary of key terms

The Actual
That which is presently given, formed, and determinate — the realized surface of what has taken form.
The Virtual
The real but not-yet-actualized field of potentials, tendencies, and intensive relations from which new actualizations emerge.
Being
The pole of form, identity, and persistence — what holds as structure (the square; capacities of being).
Becoming
The pole of transformation, openness, and difference — what moves as process (the circle; capacities of becoming).
Metastable
A tensed equilibrium charged with potential difference — neither fixed nor dissolved, poised for further individuation.
Machinic point
The fulcrum where being and becoming meet — the operational joint through which organization and individuation co-produce each other.
Processuality
The priority of becoming over finished form: reality as ongoing transformation rather than a set of completed things.
Codeterminism
Mutual determination between virtual and actual (or being and becoming): neither side is merely cause or effect; each continually remakes the other.
Inter-subjectivity
Shared meaning and coordination arising between minds — the field in which institutions and extitutions compose a common intelligence.
Institution
A closed mind: form that organizes, stabilizes, and reproduces patterns of being.
Extitution
An open mind: process that individuates, differentiates, and opens capacities of becoming.
Substrate
The material and intensive ground from which institutions and extitutions arise and into which they return.
Compose / Decompose
Twin movements of assembly toward form and dissolution back toward the virtual field.
Organization
The compositional ordering of relations into durable structure — the downward arc toward form.
Individuation
The emergence of singular beings from preindividual potentials — the upward arc of becoming.
Open-Ended Intelligence
Intelligence as a living comingle of institutional form and extitutional process — never finished, always further individuating.
