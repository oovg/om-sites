## The Open Machine is free

We are sustained by generous donations

The Open Machine is a non-profit research studio. All published media is freely available. We are sustained by generous donations that help us keep our cultural engine running.

## Donate on Open Collective

The Open Machine is fiscally sponsored by [Metagov](https://metagov.org/), a 501(c)(3) non-profit. Donors to our Open Collective account receive a receipt that allows 50% of their philanthropic contribution to be tax-deductible in participating jurisdictions.

[Donate Now →](https://opencollective.com/metagov/projects/open-machine/donate?interval=oneTime&amount=20&name=&legalName=&email=)
